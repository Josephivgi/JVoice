JVoice 0.3.2 adds a speed test that measures how fast you talk.

**New**
- Speed test: open Speed test in the sidebar, pick how long you want to talk, and press Start test. JVoice shows how fast you speak in words per minute and characters per minute.
- Pick 30 seconds, 1, 2, 5, 10 or 30 minutes, 1 hour, a custom length up to 3 hours, or No limit and press Stop when you are done. You can stop any test early.
- Your speed updates every few seconds while you talk, along with the words JVoice heard.
- The speech model writes down what you say, then JVoice counts the words and characters itself. No chat AI is used.
- Characters are letters and numbers. Spaces and punctuation are not counted, since you do not say them.
- Results are saved on your computer under Past results, with your best marked. Copy result puts your score on the clipboard.
- A test uses your OpenRouter key and costs the same as dictating for that long. While a test runs, the dictation shortcut and updates wait until it ends.

**Improved**
- Check for updates is now in the sidebar, under Settings. It shows when JVoice is checking, downloading or ready to restart, so you do not have to open Settings.
- The sidebar no longer shows a status dot and "Ready". It only shows a message when something needs fixing, such as "Shortcut not working".
- When JVoice is up to date, Settings > Updates now says which release is the newest and when it last checked, so you can see the check happened.
