JVoice 0.4.1 makes the shortcut easier to use and stops OpenRouter's content check from blocking your requests by mistake.

**Improved**
- Tap your shortcut once to talk hands-free. Recording keeps going until you tap it again, and the pill shows how to stop. Holding the keys works like before.
- Settings > Mode now shows which agent models have an OpenRouter content check. When you pick a cleanup model, the price line says if it has one.

**Fixed**
- Letting go of the keys while the microphone was still starting no longer fails with "Hold the keys a little longer". JVoice keeps recording instead.
- OpenRouter's content check sometimes blocked a harmless request, most often in Agent mode because of something on your screen. JVoice now sends each request with a backup model that has no content check, so you still get your text or your answer.
- If a block still happens in Agent mode, JVoice asks again without the screenshot.
- A content check block now says so, instead of "OpenRouter blocked this key".
